Purpose and Alignment

INNOV 11 · 20 criteria · 10 PLOs

▪How the 100 points are built

This rubric scores any student report in Medical Innovation I out of 100 points: 50 for the oral report (Part A) and 50 for the written report (Part B). Each part has 10 criteria worth 5 points each, and every criterion is tied to a Course Learning Outcome in the syllabus.

One report, two parts, twenty criteria.

▪Scope

  • Where it applies: topic reports, case analyses, journal reports and group reports in any week of either semester. The project pitch keeps its own Innovation Pitch Rubric.
  • One part only: if an activity needs only an oral or only a written report, score that part out of 50 and double it.
  • Defaults: time limits, word counts and penalties in the guidelines are suggested defaults. The faculty-in-charge may adjust them per activity and announce changes on the LMS.

All ten program outcomes are assessed at least once in each part, so a full report gives evidence for every CLO. Click an outcome or a criterion to trace its links.

Course Policy 8 · attribution of sources and tools

AI Use Policy

Up to 20% of the body · always cited

Students may use AI tools for up to 20% of the body of the written report, and every AI-assisted passage must be cited. This applies Course Policy 8, which requires attribution of sources and tools, including AI tools.

▪Is my AI use acceptable?

▪Check your AI share

AI share
AI-assisted words ÷ body word count × 100
Example: a 2,000-word body may contain at most 400 AI-assisted words.
0%20% limit40%+
  • Counted: text an AI tool drafted, paraphrased, summarized or translated that remains in the final body, even after light editing.
  • Not counted, but still listed in the disclosure form: spelling and grammar checks, brainstorming, outlining, and searching for sources.

▪How to cite

  • In text: place a citation at the end of each AI-assisted passage, for example (Developer, 2026).
  • Reference list, APA 7th: Developer. (Year). Tool name (Version) [Large language model]. URL
  • Appendix A: complete the AI Use Disclosure Form with the tool, date, prompt or purpose, location and word count for each use.

▪Limits

  • Section 8, Reflection, must be written entirely by the student.
  • The student is accountable for the accuracy of everything submitted. Verify every fact and reference an AI tool supplies.
  • AI tools are not authors and are not listed as group members.
Never enter patient-identifiable or confidential data into an AI tool (RA 10173).
AI-fabricated references or data are academic dishonesty under Course Policy 9.
